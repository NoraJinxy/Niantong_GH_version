Figure 2. (a) Grand-average ERP at Cz across motor imagery onset for stroke (n = 24) and control (n = 18) groups. Shaded region: 95% CI. (b) Topographic map of μ-band (8–13 Hz) ERD/ERS at 386 ms post-stimulus, showing reduced contralateral ERD in the affected hemisphere of stroke patients. (c) Group comparison of μ ERD magnitude in affected vs. unaffected hemispheres. Error bars: SEM. ***p < 0.001, **p < 0.01 (cluster-corrected). (d) Time-frequency representation at C3 averaged across stroke patients. Black dashed contour indicates statistically significant cluster (cluster-based permutation test, p < 0.05).
